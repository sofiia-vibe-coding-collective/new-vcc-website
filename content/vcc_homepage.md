**FAQS:**

1. **How is this different from a regular coding bootcamp or hackathon?**

We’re not trying to turn you into a developer in two hours. VCC is an exercise in collaborative making. You come along, try out some new tools, make something, get stuck, ask questions, and figure it out with other people. It’s about having a go, not getting everything right.

2. **Do I need any coding experience to attend?**

Not at all. You can come having never written a line of code. That’s a big part of why we started VCC. AI has made it possible for everyone to build things themselves, and we want to make that feel approachable rather than intimidating. 

3. **Will I be working alone or with other people?**

You’ll have your own idea to play with, but you won’t be figuring it all out alone. There’ll be members around you to answer your questions and help you get unstuck (in case you do)

4. **What if I don't finish anything by the end of the night?**

That’s completely fine. There’s no expectation or pressure to leave with a finished product. The point is to experiment, learn something, and see what you can make.

5. **Is this only for people in technical professions?**

Definitely not. We actively want to reach people outside the usual tech circles. Our events bring together people from all sorts of backgrounds, including designers, photographers, artists, marketers, founders, students, developers, and people who have never considered themselves “technical”. You don’t need to work in tech to have an idea worth building.

6. **Do I have to keep coming back, or can I just try one session?**

You can come to one event, see how you like it, and leave it at that. There’s no commitment. If you enjoy it, you can come back, try a different format, meet more people, or pick up an idea you didn’t finish the first time :) 

7. **Do I need any prior knowledge/skills to participate? and if so, what specifically.** 

There are no coding prerequisites. You should be reasonably comfortable using a computer and able to follow written instructions, but you don’t need to understand how software or AI works. What helps most is being willing to ask questions, experiment, make mistakes, and figure things out as you go. 

8. **What kind of things do people actually build?**

All sorts of things. We’ve seen people build interactive 3D experiences, tools that respond to body movements, apps that pull in live data, quirky little games, creative experiments, and tools for very specific problems. If you can imagine it, you can probably have a go at making it.

9. **Do I need to pay to attend?**

No. Our events are generally free to attend. If you’d like to contribute, you’re very welcome to make a donation. Contributions help us keep events running and make them accessible to more people.

10. **Can I join the community without attending an event?**

Yes. You can join our community through our WhatsApp groups and social media channels, meet other members, share what you’re working on, and keep up with members.
