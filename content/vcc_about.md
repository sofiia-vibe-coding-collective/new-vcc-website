**H2**: But access doesn’t always equal participation.

**Fold 2:** What are we building towards?  The cultural change we want to see  
**H1:** For years, technology made us better at scrolling, streaming, watching, and consuming things other people have made. 

**H2:** AI is (accidentally) fixing this by handing the tools of making to everyone.

**Body:** We’re interested in what happens when people start making things for themselves and others: small, strange, useful, useless, beautiful things. 

**Callout:** Or folk software, as we like to call it.

**What do you mean by “vibe coding”?**  
Vibe coding is a way of making software with AI without needing to know how to code. You can describe what you want to build, work with AI to make it, try it out, and keep refining it.

**Why focus on making rather than just learning about AI?**  
Because you learn differently when you’re actually trying to make something. Instead of just learning what AI can do, you get to find out what it can do for you.
