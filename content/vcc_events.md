FAQs.    
**Do I need to bring my own laptop?**  
Yes\! Bring your laptop with you. It’s what you’ll use to build, experiment, and hopefully surprise yourself with what you manage to make**.**

**What happens if I don’t finish my project?**  
Nothing\! There’s no pressure to finish. The point is to have a go, learn something, and see where you can take an idea. You can always keep building after the event.

**What can I do with my project after the event?**  
Whatever you like. Keep tinkering with it, share it with friends, use it at work, or turn it into something bigger. We’ll point you towards resources if you want to keep going.

**Can I deploy or share what I’ve built?**  
Absolutely. If you want to put your project on Github or share it with others, we can help you figure out what to do next.

**How do I know if what I’ve built actually works?**  
The best way is to try it\! You’ll test things as you go, fix what breaks, and ask questions along the way. You definitely don’t need to understand every line of code.

**What if I get stuck?**  
You almost certainly will, at some point. That’s part of the fun. There’ll be people around to help you out, and asking someone nearby is very much encouraged.

**Is there a cost to attend?**  
Our events are generally free. If you’d like to contribute, you’re very welcome to donate. It helps us keep events running and accessible to more people.

**Can I come on my own?**  
Of course. You won’t be expected to arrive knowing anyone. Come on your own and we’ll help you find your way into the room.
