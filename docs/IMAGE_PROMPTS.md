# Supplementary image generation

Tool: built-in ImageGen. Reference: the user-supplied photographic moodboard, used only for atmosphere and camera treatment. All depicted people are fictional. Each image is labelled “Illustrative image” in the website.

## cafe-builders.webp

Wide 3:2 candid documentary photograph of four fictional adult friends from varied backgrounds working together on laptops around a wooden table in a dim warm London cafe at night. Relaxed stylish casual clothing, laughter, someone pointing at a screen, imperfect framing, intimate and inviting, amber practical lights and direct on-camera flash, analog grain and authentic textures. Realistic hands and laptop hardware, no legible text, no brand marks. For a community website, not a corporate stock photo.

## overhead-builders.webp

Portrait 4:5 candid documentary photograph from an unusual overhead angle of three fictional adult friends collaborating around laptops and a notebook at a warm wood cafe table, one person perched sideways on a chair, casual creative clothing, natural candid expressions. Amber dim venue lighting with direct flash, subtle analog grain, warm brown and cream tones. Authentic inviting creative community atmosphere. Realistic hands and laptop hardware. No legible text, no brand marks, no sticky notes or collage graphics.

## after-workshop.webp

Wide 3:2 candid documentary photograph of a small group of fictional adult friends laughing and talking after a creative coding workshop in a dim pub with warm amber light, a laptop and notebooks on the edge of a wood table, spontaneous lively emotions, stylish casual clothes, one person caught mid-gesture. Direct on-camera flash, slightly offbeat composition, analog grain, deep warm shadows, authentic convivial community atmosphere. Realistic faces and hands. No legible text, no brand marks.

Shared instruction: use the attached image only as a photographic mood reference: candid, warm, dim rooms, flash and unconventional angles. Generate one standalone photograph, never the reference collage or its writing.

Project outputs are WebP at quality 85, maximum landscape size 1536 × 1024 and portrait size 1024 × 1280. Original generated PNGs remain in the ImageGen output directory.
